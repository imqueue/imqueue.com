# @imqueue — commercial license & support

Source: https://imqueue.com/

{{ site.definition }}

imqueue.com is the **commercial** side of @imqueue: licensing and paid support.
The framework itself, its documentation, tutorial, CLI guide and full API
reference are open source and live at <https://imqueue.org>.

## What this site is for

- [Pricing & commercial license](https://imqueue.com/pricing/) — lift the GPL-3.0
  source-release obligation and add SLA-backed support.
- [Licensing explained](https://imqueue.com/license/) — how the dual licence
  works, and a decision rule for which side of it you are on.
- [Commercial support & SLAs](https://imqueue.com/support/) — guaranteed response
  times, direct maintainer access, upgrade help and indemnification.
- [Contact](https://imqueue.com/contact/) — anything that is not a licensing quote.

## The packages are identical either way

There is one build of `@imqueue/*`. A commercial licence changes the **terms**,
not the code: no fork, no separate distribution, no migration, and the same APIs.
You can prototype on the open-source edition and license later without changing a
line.
